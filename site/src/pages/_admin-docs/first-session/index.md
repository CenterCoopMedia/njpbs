## Before you start

Allow about 15 minutes with your manager or an experienced editor. You need an
approved work account, an assigned practice draft, and the name of your reviewer.
Ask your manager to choose a draft that can safely contain training changes.
Keep this guide open beside Boardwalk.

The screenshots show Boardwalk rendered locally with sample records. Names,
counts, dates, artwork, and statuses are examples. They do not show current
staff assignments or a live publication. Your role and deployed Boardwalk version
may show different controls. Follow the text if an image is hard to read, or
select an image to open it at full size.

If you have staff access without an editorial role, you can read **Broadcast
schedule**, use the staff media sources available to you, and read this guide.
Ask your manager to arrange the editorial access your job needs.

Use [Access and roles](../access-and-roles/) for Boardwalk sign-in, first-time
access, password recovery, and the **Preparing your workspace** checklist.

### 1. Find your way around

Sign in to <a href="https://admin.njpbs.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Boardwalk<span class="sr-only"> (opens in new tab)</span></a>. Check your name in the account
menu. Find **Broadcast schedule**, which is available to every signed-in
account. Find **Pages**, **Posts**, **All content**, and **Media library** if
your account has access to them. Use this guide for the staff procedures.

<figure class="admin-docs-screenshot">
  <p class="admin-docs-image-note">Screenshot omitted from this source distribution.</p>
  <figcaption><strong>Start with the navigation.</strong> Choose a workspace on the left, or reopen a record from Recent work. Your role may show fewer destinations. </figcaption>
</figure>

Home has two personal shortcuts. Editorial accounts start with **My recent edits**
and **Assigned to me**. The other choices are **Browse media** and **Open guide**,
when your access permits. Guide-only accounts keep their guide landing page. Select **Customize Home shortcuts**, choose two distinct
destinations, check the preview, then select **Save shortcuts**. The choices follow
your account across devices. **Reset to defaults** restores the choices for your
current role. Shortcuts do not change your access; unavailable choices fall back
to permitted destinations when your role changes.

Recent work shows workflow status and public-copy status below the title. A draft
can still have an older published copy live. Read both statuses before editing.

**Check:** You can name the workspace for your assignment. Pages holds site
pages; Posts holds individual stories and updates; All content searches across
records you can access. Do not create a duplicate if the record already exists.

### 2. Open the assigned draft

Open **All content**. Search for a short part of the title. Open **Filters** if
you need to narrow by type or status. Select the record title and check its
identity before editing. If you see **Edit**, select it to enter editing mode.

<figure class="admin-docs-screenshot">
  <p class="admin-docs-image-note">Screenshot omitted from this source distribution.</p>
  <figcaption><strong>Search, then narrow the results.</strong> Filters changes the result set; selecting a title opens the record. The Workflow and Public columns describe different states. </figcaption>
</figure>

**Check:** The title matches the assignment and the record is the practice draft
your manager chose. Clear unnecessary filters if it is missing. Do not select
row checkboxes; those are for bulk actions.

### 3. Make one small change

Find **Title and summary** or the equivalent basics section. Make the agreed
copy change. Use **Post body** to reach the content blocks and **Post settings**
for byline, dates, search, and sharing fields. Leave unrelated fields alone.

<figure class="admin-docs-screenshot">
  <p class="admin-docs-image-note">Screenshot omitted from this source distribution.</p>
  <figcaption><strong>Save draft keeps your work in the CMS.</strong> Publish is a separate action. Title and summary, Post body, and Post settings help you move through a long editor. </figcaption>
</figure>

**Check:** Read the changed sentence with the text around it. If you added an
image, complete the [image walkthrough](../metadata-and-media/#add-an-image-to-a-post)
before saving. Ask for a handoff if another editor holds the record.

### 4. Save and check the result

Select **Save draft** once and wait for confirmation. Resolve any highlighted
field errors. Once saving finishes, reload the record and confirm the change
remains.

**Check:** The saved field matches your change. Saving a draft has not published
it. Stop at this point for a practice session; do not select **Publish**.

### 5. Leave without losing work

If a warning appears when you leave, read the choices before continuing.

<figure class="admin-docs-screenshot">
  <p class="admin-docs-image-note">Screenshot omitted from this source distribution.</p>
  <figcaption><strong>Choose what happens to your unsaved work.</strong> Keep editing returns to the draft. Save and leave waits for a successful save. Discard and leave loses the unsaved changes. </figcaption>
</figure>

**Check:** Use **Keep editing** if you still need to work. Use **Save and leave**
when the change is complete and that option is available. Use **Discard and
leave** only for a change you intentionally want to lose.

### 6. Hand the draft to your reviewer

Use your team's agreed review channel. Include the record title and Boardwalk link,
what changed, and what the reviewer should check. Confirm who will publish a
real assignment and who will check the public page afterward.

**Check:** Your reviewer can find the saved draft. A Boardwalk link opens the editor;
it is not a public article link or an exact-draft preview.

## A checklist for the person leading training

Before ending the session, ask the new staff member to show you how to:

- Find the assigned record without creating a duplicate.
- Explain the difference between the working draft and the public version.
- Change one field, save, and confirm it after reloading.
- Find the image picker and explain alt text, caption, and credit.
- Return safely from an unsaved-work warning.
- Find **Report a problem**.

Record any access or training follow-up in your team's normal training plan.
Use the browser's Print command to print this walkthrough or save it as a PDF.
