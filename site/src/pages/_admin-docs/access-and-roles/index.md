## Sign in

Open <a href="https://admin.njpbs.org/" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Boardwalk<span class="sr-only"> (opens in new tab)</span></a> in a current browser. The NJ PBS station logo identifies the service.

Use the exact work account approved for you. If that account ends in
`@montclair.edu`, start with **Continue with Google** and select that account.
Do not choose a personal Google identity. Existing users can still use the
email-and-password sign-in and reset routes.

Boardwalk supports the account methods shown on screen:

- Continue with Google.
- Email and password.
- A first-time account request followed by email verification and approval.

Do not switch between personal and work Google accounts in the same session
without signing out first. If the wrong account opens, sign out and restart the
flow with the approved address.

The **Show password** control changes only whether you can see the current
Password field. It does not change the saved password. Boardwalk hides the
password again when you switch between **Sign in** and **Create account** or
start an account action.

Boardwalk disables the account-mode controls while sign-in, account creation,
or password recovery is pending. Wait for the result before you switch modes or
submit another request.

## Preparing your workspace

During entry, Boardwalk can show **Preparing your workspace**. The checklist
follows four real phases:

1. **Restoring your sign-in**
2. **Confirming staff access**
3. **Loading your profile and permissions**
4. **Opening your workspace**

A check identifies a phase that finished. The current line identifies the work
in progress, and later lines wait their turn. The checklist is not a progress
percentage. A decorative Boardwalk tip can change while one phase continues.

During an ordinary wait, Boardwalk tips change every 4.5 seconds. They start at
a random tip without an immediate repeat. Tips do not add checks or delay entry.

Select **Pause text** if you need more time to read. Boardwalk holds the current
tip while the real phase and slow-wait status continue to update. **Resume text**
remains available while paused. A reduced-motion setting or a hidden browser
tab also stops automatic tip changes. Screen readers announce the real status
without the decorative tips.

After eight seconds, tips stop rotating and Boardwalk says the current check is
still pending. Record the active checklist phase and slow message. The tip is
not diagnostic evidence. Do not force a refresh until you have preserved work
in any other Boardwalk tab and confirmed that it is safe to restart entry.

## Request first-time access

When **Create account** is available:

1. Enter the name colleagues should recognize.
2. Enter the work email address you intend to keep using.
3. Create a password that meets the requirement shown on screen.
4. Explain why you need access and what work you expect to do.
5. Submit the request.
6. Open the verification message and use its link.
7. Return to Boardwalk and wait for an administrator to approve or reject the request. The administrator is
   the only account-request approver.

Verification proves ownership of the email address. It does not grant CMS
permissions by itself.

When a verified request reaches the decision queue, Boardwalk sends a staff
notification. An administrator makes the decision in Boardwalk.

A request should describe the work, not include private material. For example:
“Edit assigned NJ PBS program posts and select approved media” is useful.
Passwords, donor records, member information, contracts, and unpublished
editorial details are not.

## Understand the role model

Permissions are based on capabilities. An area grant limits which pages and posts
someone can edit: Watch, Support, Station or Compliance. Categories file media;
they are separate from areas. Media access still depends on the account's existing
permissions in those categories.

| Role | One line | Can | Cannot |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Viewer | Can read the guide and browse authorized audio. Cannot change content. | Read the private Boardwalk guide, browse audio in authorized categories, file problem reports and read their own reports. | Create, edit or publish content; upload or manage media; manage the schedule, settings, staff accounts or access. |
| Writer | Can draft and edit their own work. An Editor publishes it. | Read the guide, browse authorized audio, draft and edit their own work in their areas, and manage their own profile and profile photo. | Edit Home (including a draft they own) or other people's work, publish pages or posts, delete content, manage media or upload beyond their own profile photo, or manage the schedule, settings, staff accounts or access. |
| Editor | Can edit and publish any work in their areas, and manage media in authorized categories. | Read the guide, browse authorized audio, draft, edit, publish and unpublish work in their areas, and upload and manage media in authorized categories. | Delete content or manage the schedule, settings, staff accounts or access. |
| Manager | Can also delete content, manage the schedule and settings, view staff accounts and manage staff profiles. | Do everything an Editor can do; delete content; manage the broadcast schedule and site settings; view staff accounts and manage staff profiles; view member records and administrative activity. | Approve account requests, deactivate staff accounts, grant or revoke roles, or change their own administrative role. |
| Site owner | Can do everything available in Boardwalk, including give access. | Do everything a Manager can do and grant or revoke staff roles and access to areas. | Change their own administrative role, revoke their own roles or remove the last Site owner. |

Home requires Editor access in the Station area or a higher role with that
access. An Editor limited to another area cannot edit Home.

The Staff access roster and approval picker explain each role's permissions in
**Can** and **Cannot** lists.

**Publish** requires publishing permission as well as edit access to the content.
Its presence does not replace your team's review and publishing agreement. See
[review, preview, and publish](../review-preview-and-publish/).

The safest access is the least access that supports the person's job. Do not ask
for a Manager role simply to make a missing editorial control appear.

## Why a destination or control may be missing

Boardwalk filters both navigation and actions. A control can be absent because:

- Your role does not include that capability.
- Your account is limited to an area or set of records.
- The record is submitted, published, archived, or otherwise read-only for your
  role.
- Another prerequisite has not been met.
- The action belongs to a reviewer or administrator rather than the author.
- The account was changed and the current session has not refreshed its claims.

First confirm the assignment and role. Then reload Boardwalk or sign out and back
in after a known role change. Do not work around a missing control with a
different account.

## Pending, rejected, and inactive access

A pending account should show a clear status instead of opening Boardwalk.
Wait for the approval process or contact the CMS administrator through the
established support channel.

A rejected or inactive account must not be reused. If access is still required,
ask an administrator to review the current business need. Do not create
duplicate accounts to bypass the prior decision.

If your email identity changes, Boardwalk may refuse the account because it no
longer matches the approved identity. Sign out and use the approved address, or
have an administrator update the access record.

## Reset a password

On the email-and-password sign-in screen:

1. Enter the account email.
2. Select **Forgot password?**
3. Read the confirmation. For privacy, it may not disclose whether an account
   exists.
4. Use the newest reset message only.
5. Return to Boardwalk and sign in again.

Never forward a reset or verification link. Treat it as a credential.

If you use Google sign-in, recover the Google account through the organization
that manages it rather than creating a second Boardwalk identity.

## Role changes

After an approved role or area change:

1. Finish or discard any unsaved work.
2. Sign out.
3. Sign back in with the same approved account.
4. Confirm that the expected destinations now appear.
5. Open one assigned record and verify the expected read or edit state.
6. Report a mismatch with the account email, intended role, expected
   destination, and exact message.

Site owners should record why access changed and prefer a narrow area
grant when it meets the need. Remove access when the work ends.

## Sign out safely

Use **Sign out** from the account menu. If Boardwalk warns about unsaved work,
return to the editor and either save or deliberately discard it before ending
the session.

Sign out when:

- You are using a shared or temporary device.
- You need to switch accounts.
- Your role has just changed.
- You have completed sensitive administrative work.
- The displayed identity is not yours.

Closing the tab is not a reliable substitute for signing out on a shared
device.

## Analytics access

Only Managers and Site owners can read the site Analytics dashboard. Other staff
roles do not receive this reporting access, including through a direct link.
