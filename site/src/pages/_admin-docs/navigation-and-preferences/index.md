Boardwalk keeps one persistent shell around every workspace. It contains:

- The NJ PBS lockup.
- The rail.
- The current workspace title and status.
- Page-specific actions.
- View options.
- The account menu.
- The main content region.

When you move between destinations, the shell remains in place while the active
workspace changes. Wait for the loading status to finish before interpreting an
empty or partial view.

## Use Home as the starting point

**Home** summarizes editorial work for accounts with access to that workspace.
Guide-only accounts keep their guide landing page. Use Home to orient yourself
before editing:

1. Confirm the account and newsroom identity.
2. Read recent editorial work.
3. Review any system or release status shown there.
4. Open the next task from its record link, or choose a destination in the
   rail.

Home is a summary, not a substitute for the record itself. Open the record and
read its current lifecycle, assignment, and saved state before acting.

### Choose your Home shortcuts

Select **Customize Home shortcuts** beside the two top actions. Each slot offers
only destinations you can currently use. Choose two different destinations and
check **Preview** before you select **Save shortcuts**. The choices are saved to
your account, like your rail preferences, and follow you across devices.

Editorial defaults are **My recent edits** and **Assigned to me**. The available
alternatives are **Browse media** and **Open guide**. **Reset to defaults** clears
your saved choices. If your access changes, Home replaces unavailable shortcuts
with permitted defaults. A shortcut never grants access.

**New post** and **Edit homepage** remain in the secondary Shortcuts panel when
your role permits them. Recent work shows workflow and public-copy status below
each title; these can differ when a live record has a newer draft.

## Understand the navigation rail

A destination is a built-in entry. A plank is one link you add. A span is a named group of planks. Shared planks are planks a staff member has shared. Each person turns a shared plank on, then saves their own rail.

The default rail can include:

- Home
- Pages, Posts, All content, and Media library, grouped as Content
- Editorial queue, Activity, and Broadcast schedule, grouped as Workflow
- Resources, Staff profiles, and Staff access, grouped as Team
- Analytics, Reports, and Admin settings, grouped as Administration
- Around NJ

Inside **Media library**, choose a tab: **Images**, **Videos**, **Public submissions**,
**Audio**, **Favorites** or **Collections**. **Images** also holds PDF
documents. **Public submissions** holds files from the public and appears only for site-wide
media managers. Tabs are links, so Enter opens the focused tab. An existing
`/voiceovers` bookmark opens Media library with the **Audio** tab selected.

The order and available destinations depend on your account. Capability rules
decide what may appear. Personal arrangement settings can change the order or
hide an eligible destination, but they do not expand or reduce the underlying
authorization.

**Broadcast schedule** is a required, read-only destination for every signed-in
account. It states the source packet, coverage dates, and Eastern time zone. It
does not use or replace the public PBS listings feed. If the requested week is
outside the verified coverage, use the closest covered week and report the data
gap to an administrator.

If you cannot find a workspace:

1. Open the account menu and confirm your identity.
2. Expand the rail if it is collapsed.
3. Check whether you previously hid or rearranged the destination.
4. Confirm that the destination is appropriate for your role.
5. Sign out and back in after a known access change.
6. Report the mismatch if the expected destination is still absent.

Do not assume another person's rail should match yours.

## Collapse and restore the rail

On a wide screen, the rail can be collapsed to leave more room for the editor.
The CMS remembers this display preference.

A collapsed rail retains accessible names and recognizable controls. Hover,
focus, or use the account's text-label setting when you need more explicit
labels. Restoring the rail does not change content or permissions.

On a narrow screen, use the menu control to open and close navigation. The menu
also groups the view options and account controls below the navigation
destinations. It should close after you choose a destination. If it covers the
workspace after navigation, close it with the menu control or <kbd>Escape</kbd>
when available.

## Use view options

The view-options control changes information density without changing the
record.

| Density | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| Compact | Seeing more rows or controls at once on a large display. |
| Comfortable | The default balance for most editorial work. |
| Loose | More separation between controls, useful for touch or visual scanning. |

Try each density with a real work surface. Choose the one that gives you enough
context without making controls difficult to target. The preference persists
for the account or browser according to the setting shown in Admin.

## Set control presentation

**Account settings** can offer text-label and icon-oriented control
presentations. Required labels and accessible names remain available in either
mode, but the visual density differs. The text-label choice applies on wider
screens. On a phone, controls such as **Upload** and **Filter** always show only
the icon.

Use text labels when:

- You are learning the CMS.
- Several actions have similar symbols.
- You work intermittently and do not want to memorize the icon set.
- You need the clearest possible review or training environment.

Use the approved icon presentation when you already understand the actions and
need a more compact workspace. Never infer a high-risk action from its symbol
alone; check its accessible name, current status, and confirmation text.

## Use the account menu

The account menu contains personal actions rather than newsroom destinations.
Use it to open:

- **Profile** for your public byline and biography.
- **Settings** for your personal display choices.
- **Sign out** to end the session safely.

The displayed name, email, initials, or profile photograph should match the
person using the CMS. Stop and sign out if they do not.

## See what is new in Boardwalk

The gift icon in the header opens **What's new**. It lists recent changes to
Boardwalk in plain language, newest first, grouped by month. Each note says
whether something is new, improved or fixed.

A small dot on the gift icon means there are notes you have not seen. The dot
clears after the notes load while the dialog is open. A failed load keeps the dot.
On Home, select the gift icon directly, including on a phone. In editors, open
**More**. On Pages, Posts, Media library and the queue, open **Help**. Select
**What's new** beside the Boardwalk guide and Report a problem.

## Return to the right place

After you sign in, public Home, Page, Post, Program and Event pages show
the **Edit in Boardwalk** bar. Select anywhere on the bar to open that record
in its editor. Its label is centered at every screen width, in a separate row
above the site header. It hides while the navigation or cookie choice dialog
is open.

If your session has ended, Boardwalk asks you to sign in and keeps the target.
Your current permissions still apply. An unavailable record or a record you
cannot edit shows a plain message. Writers cannot edit Home through this link.
The dev public site opens dev Boardwalk; the production site opens production
Boardwalk. Search results, content indexes and author pages have no single
content record and do not show the action.

The CMS can remember the last eligible destination used in a tab. After a
refresh or sign-in, it may restore that workspace when the address and your
permissions allow it.

A restored destination is a convenience, not proof that the record is still
assigned to you. Reconfirm the title, status, and assignment after a long pause,
a role change, or a new editorial shift.

## Navigate with keyboard and focus

The shell manages focus when a route changes and announces the new destination
to assistive technology.

- Use <kbd>Tab</kbd> and <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>Tab</kbd> to move through
  controls.
- Use the skip link at the start of the page to move directly to the main
  workspace.
- Use <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd> for the focused button according to
  normal browser behavior.
- Use arrow keys inside controls that identify themselves as a menu,
  radiogroup, or other composite widget.
- Use <kbd>Escape</kbd> to close an open menu or dialog when the control supports
  it.

A visible focus ring is part of the interface. Do not remove it with browser
extensions or custom style overrides.

## Work on a phone-sized screen

At a narrow width:

1. Open the main menu.
2. Choose the destination.
3. Close the menu if it remains open.
4. Use the content outline or section headings to move through a long editor.
5. Expect icon-only controls. Controls such as **Upload** and **Filter** show
   only the icon on a phone.
6. Keep primary save and lifecycle actions visible before making a consequential
   change.
7. Rotate the device only when necessary; the CMS should remain usable in either
   orientation.

Report any horizontal scrolling, clipped action, unreachable field, or target
that is difficult to tap. Include the approximate viewport width and browser.

## Arrange your navigation step by step

Open **Account settings** from the account menu and find **Your rail**.

<figure class="admin-docs-screenshot">
  <p class="admin-docs-image-note">Screenshot omitted from this source distribution.</p>
  <figcaption><strong>Arrange your rail.</strong> Grip handles and Move controls change order; Hide removes an eligible destination from your rail. Save rail applies the arrangement. </figcaption>
</figure>

1. Drag a destination's grip to change its position. On touch, hold the grip before dragging; scroll from the rest of the row. With the grip focused, press Up or Down. Boardwalk announces the new position and keeps focus on the grip. Escape cancels a drag. Open **Move** for **Move up**, **Move down**, **Move to first** or **Move to last**. Options that cannot change the current position are omitted.
2. Use **Hide** only for an optional destination you seldom need. In icon mode, use its named eye control. Hidden destinations stay in this list with a **Show** action.
3. Check **Unsaved changes** in the footer, select **Save rail**, and check the rail. **Cancel changes** restores the last saved arrangement. A failed save keeps your changes available to retry.
4. Return here to show a hidden destination or use **Reset to default**. If you have
   planks or spans, confirm that reset removes them and restores the built-in order.
   Save and Cancel sit below the list. Reset is separate from these routine actions.
   Scroll the page to reach the footer.

To add a plank, select **Add to your rail**. Choose **Plank**, enter a short label,
and choose a CMS path, an HTTPS address, a post, or a page. Use the content search
and select a result for a post or page. These planks store the content ID, so a title
or slug change does not break the plank. External links open in a new tab.
A deleted or unavailable post shows the editor's not-found message when opened.

Choose **Span** to group your own planks. Add planks, then use their move, edit,
hide, or delete controls. Select **Apply plank** or **Apply span**, then **Save rail** in the footer. Cancel discards those changes. Move and hide planks and spans with the
same controls as built-in destinations. Delete removes a plank or span after you save.
There is a limit of ten planks and spans; each span and each plank counts as one.
Only this account's rail changes. Access checks still apply to every plank.

**Check:** Your arrangement changed; your access did not. Home, Broadcast schedule,
and required administration destinations cannot be hidden. If a workspace is
still absent, check your role rather than repeatedly resetting the layout.

## Read site analytics

Managers and Site owners can open **Analytics** in the rail. It reports
site audience, newsletters, and website delivery. [Read the analytics guide](../analytics/)
for metric definitions and reporting limits. **Reports** remains the CMS problem-report workspace.
