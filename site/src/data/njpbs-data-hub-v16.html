A higher minimum wage is coming in January

The announced rate for most New Jersey workers is $16.48 an hour starting Jan. 1, 2027 — 56 cents above the 2026 rate. Different rules apply to tipped workers and several other groups.

    Reading the numbers

    A statewide figure does not describe every county or town. Local comparisons appear only when we can account for every place in that geographic level; unavailable values remain visible, not quietly omitted. Check the “Definition and notes” link under any measure.

    Comparisons always use matching definitions and reporting periods. Property tax bills are not rates; permits are not completed homes; survey estimates include uncertainty. Source changes enter editorial review rather than automatically replacing published data. NJ TRANSIT performance is held until reuse rights are cleared.
