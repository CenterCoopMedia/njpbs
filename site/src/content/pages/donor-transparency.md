## Donor rights

NJ PBS respects the privacy of everyone who supports public media in New Jersey.
If you give to NJ PBS, you have the right to:

- know what donor information we hold and how it is used;
- ask us to correct or update your information;
- ask us to delete your information, subject to the records we keep for tax,
  audit, and legal reasons;
- receive a copy of the information you have given us; and
- opt out of marketing contact at any time.

You exercise any of these by contacting us at the address at the bottom of this
page. We do not ask you to give a reason.

## How your records are used

NJ PBS uses donor information to process your gift, send your receipt and tax
acknowledgment, thank you, keep you informed about your membership and our
programming, and meet our reporting and legal obligations.

We do not collect or store payment-card numbers on this website. Donations are
processed through PBS-hosted, Stripe-backed forms, and your card details stay
with that payment processor. Your name, contact details, and giving history are
kept in our donor records system, not on this website. We collect only what we
need for the purposes above. We limit access to staff whose work requires it.

## We do not share your information with candidates or political committees

NJ PBS does not share, rent, exchange, or sell donor or contributor lists with
political candidates, political parties, or political action committees. We keep
active control of our contributor lists, and we do not disclose donor names to
unaffiliated third parties without giving you advance notice and a chance to opt
out.

This is the donor-protection standard Congress wrote for public broadcasting in
47 U.S.C. 396(k)(12).

## NJ PBS operations

Montclair State University became the operator of NJ PBS. See the
[public NJ PBS FAQ](https://njpbs.org/faq/) for viewer and membership updates.

## Contact

[njpbs@montclair.edu](mailto:njpbs@montclair.edu?subject=NJ%20PBS%20donor%20privacy%20request).
That inbox is the intake point for donor-privacy requests.
