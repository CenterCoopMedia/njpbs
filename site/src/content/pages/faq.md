## NJ PBS operations

### What is changing at NJ PBS?

Beginning July 1, 2026, Montclair State University is the new operator of NJ PBS.
During the next three months, Montclair will hire staff, set up systems, and
prepare programming for the next phase of NJ PBS.

### Who is the new operator of NJ PBS?

Montclair State University is the new operator of NJ PBS.

### What was WNET's role?

WNET operated NJ PBS from July 1, 2011, until June 30, 2026.

### Why is this transition happening?

WNET's contract to operate NJ PBS ended June 30, 2026. Montclair State
University became the new operator on July 1, 2026. Montclair is using the
summer to hire staff, set up systems, and prepare programming. We appreciate
your patience and support during this transition.

## Viewing and Passport

### Is anything changing immediately for viewers?

Nothing will change immediately during the transition period. Viewers can
continue watching NJ PBS, and eligible members will retain PBS Passport while
Montclair sets up the next phase of NJ PBS.

### Does my existing access to PBS Passport continue without interruption?

PBS manages Passport account status. Use the
<a href="https://www.pbs.org/passport/lookup/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">PBS Passport lookup tool<span class="sr-only"> (opens in new tab)</span></a>
to check an existing account. If access does not appear or a renewal is due,
contact NJ PBS for help with the current account or donation path.

### Do I need to do anything right now?

You do not need an account for immediate broadcast viewing. For Passport, check
your account through PBS and contact NJ PBS if access or renewal information is
not shown correctly.

Existing gifts, including one-time and recurring gifts, ended June 30, 2026. If
you would like to continue supporting NJ PBS, you will need to set up a new
monthly recurring gift or make a one-time donation through the new donation
portal. You can also donate by mail. Make checks payable to NJ PBS and mail
them to NJ PBS, 1 Normal Ave., Montclair, NJ 07043. A mailed check supports NJ
PBS but does not activate PBS Passport; to start or renew Passport streaming,
donate through the new donation portal.

## Donations and renewals

### What happens to my recurring donation?

Existing recurring gifts ended June 30, 2026. Members who want to continue a
monthly gift to NJ PBS will need to set up a new monthly recurring gift through
the new donation portal.

### Where can I renew or make a donation?

You can renew or make a donation through the new NJ PBS donation portal:
<a href="https://www.pbs.org/donation/?station_id=63b643e1-1a13-4dd9-9bab-ff6f7527715f&campaign=passport" rel="noopener" target="_blank">PBS donation portal<span class="sr-only"> (opens in new tab)</span></a>

### Can I donate by mailing a check?

Yes. Make checks payable to NJ PBS and mail them to:

NJ PBS<br />
1 Normal Ave.<br />
Montclair, NJ 07043

### Will donors need to re-enter payment information?

Yes, for online gifts. Donors giving online will need to enter payment
information in the new donation form. Mailing a check skips that step.

### What happens to my prior giving history and tax receipts?

WNET maintains giving history and tax receipts for all donations made before
July 1, 2026.

## Data, preferences, and help

### How is my personal or payment information handled?

For online gifts, payment information is entered through the PBS donation portal
and processed by its payment processor. A mailed check goes to NJ PBS in
Montclair and does not pass through that portal. Member and Passport records are
handled through PBS membership systems. This website does not collect
payment-card information.
