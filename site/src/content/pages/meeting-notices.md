NJ PBS links to the official public-meeting notice pages for the public bodies
connected to the station. Those source pages carry the current schedules,
agendas, minutes, and updates.

## NJPBA public meeting notices

The New Jersey Public Broadcasting Authority posts its meeting schedules, agendas,
and minutes on its official page:

- <a href="https://www.nj.gov/treasury/njpba/minutes.shtml" rel="noopener" target="_blank">NJPBA public meetings<span class="sr-only"> (opens in new tab)</span></a>

## Montclair State University public meeting notices

The Montclair State University board of trustees posts its agenda and schedule on
its official page:

- <a href="https://www.montclair.edu/board-of-trustees/agenda-and-schedule/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">Montclair State University board of trustees agenda and schedule<span class="sr-only"> (opens in new tab)</span></a>

## Open Public Meetings Act

New Jersey's Open Public Meetings Act requires public bodies to give advance
public notice of their meetings. For the latest details, use the official pages
linked above.
