This notice explains how NJ PBS handles information on this website. Montclair
State University also publishes a
<a href="https://www.montclair.edu/privacy-notice/" rel="noopener" target="_blank">website privacy notice<span class="sr-only"> (opens in new tab)</span></a>
for university-owned websites, services, applications, and technology products.

## Data collected

NJ PBS and its hosting services may process limited technical information needed
to operate and secure the website, including browser type, device type,
approximate connection information, and the date and time of a request. If you
allow analytics, Google Analytics also records information about the pages you
visit and how you reached the site. When you submit a site search with analytics
allowed, Google Analytics receives the words you searched for and the number of
results. If you email NJ PBS or submit a form, the information you provide is
used to respond to your request.

## Analytics and cookies

NJ PBS uses Google Analytics 4 to understand site traffic and improve this
website. The Google tag does not load, and no analytics request or analytics
cookie is created, unless you select **Allow analytics and newsletter tools**.
Advertising storage, ad user data, ad personalization, and Google signals stay
off.

The same choice controls Mailchimp's connected-site code, which supports NJ PBS
newsletter signup tools. Mailchimp code does not load until you allow it. If NJ
PBS publishes a Mailchimp signup form, Mailchimp may use a cookie to remember
whether you viewed the form and may measure form performance.

The same choice also controls ActiveCampaign site tracking on eligible pages.
ActiveCampaign records page visits and IP addresses for newsletter marketing and
may associate visits with an existing contact record. This integration does not
send an email address or call ActiveCampaign's identification features. Its code
does not load until you allow these tools. Visitors with an older saved choice
are asked again before any of these tools load.

The same choice controls Wisepops, which NJ PBS uses to display newsletter
signup forms and messages. If you allow it, Wisepops may use first-party cookies
to remember which forms and messages you viewed or submitted and may process
your IP address and country. Information that you enter in a Wisepops form, such
as your email address, is sent to Wisepops and may be sent to a newsletter
service that NJ PBS connects to Wisepops.

The site saves your choice in the first-party `njpbs-consent` cookie. The browser
uses this record to enforce your choice. A denial or Global Privacy Control
signal keeps Google Analytics, Mailchimp, ActiveCampaign and Wisepops off. NJ PBS
excludes these tools from Kids and its descendant pages, content marked as
child-directed, and pages that play video. ActiveCampaign and Wisepops also stay
off previews, development pages, pages excluded from search-engine indexing and
redirect stubs. Changing your choice to **Do not allow** stops future loading.
If ActiveCampaign or Wisepops has loaded in this page, the page reloads to end
its running code. This cannot recall information already sent. You can request
deletion using the contact below.

## Donation and Passport data

NJ PBS does not collect payment-card information on this site. Online donations
are processed through PBS donation tools; a donation mailed as a check goes
directly to NJ PBS and does not pass through those tools. PBS Passport viewing
and account sign-in are handled by PBS-hosted services.

## Your privacy choices

You can ask NJ PBS to:

- tell you what personal information it holds about you, and give you a copy
- correct information that is wrong
- delete personal information it holds about you
- opt out of any sale of your personal information, or its use for targeted
  advertising

Email [njpbs@montclair.edu](mailto:njpbs@montclair.edu) and say which of these
you are asking for.

You can also manage cookies through your browser settings. Some features may not
work as expected if cookies are blocked.
