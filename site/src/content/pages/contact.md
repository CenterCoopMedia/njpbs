Use this page to see NJ PBS contact information before choosing how to get in
touch. General help links on this site point here so a browser does not open a
mail app automatically.

## How to contact NJ PBS

- Email: [njpbs@montclair.edu](mailto:njpbs@montclair.edu?subject=NJ%20PBS%20question)
- Phone: [800-882-6622](tel:+18008826622)

Mailing address:<br />
NJ PBS<br />
1 Normal Ave.<br />
Montclair, NJ 07043

Use the email address or phone number above for viewer questions, membership
questions, PBS Passport help, donation questions, and questions about this
website.

For press and media inquiries, contact [njpbs@montclair.edu](mailto:njpbs@montclair.edu?subject=Press%20inquiry).

## When to use this page

Contact NJ PBS if you need help with:

- Watching NJ PBS live or on demand.
- PBS Passport access or account lookup.
- Renewing, starting, or changing a donation.
- Finding the right page for schedule, public file, accessibility, or captioning
  information.

If your question involves a PBS-hosted service, such as the video player,
Passport sign-in, the PBS app, or the donation form, NJ PBS will help route the
question to the right support path.
