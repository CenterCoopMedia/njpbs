# Editorial independence and public accountability

Editorial independence was part of the public discussion of
[Montclair State University's selection](selection-2026.md) to operate NJ PBS.
The state's June 3, 2026, announcement said the university had committed to a
journalism ethics code, an independent ethics committee with external
appointments and a Community Advisory Board. The announcement described
commitments, rather than documenting completed appointments or a published code.
[Treasury's selection announcement](https://www.nj.gov/treasury/news/2026/06032026.shtml).

## Ethics committee nominations

NJ PBS subsequently invited nominations for an Ethics and Journalistic Standards
Committee. Its public notice described a group of 10 to 12 people that would
work with station staff to establish an ethics and integrity code, develop a
complaint process and review ethical concerns. The notice proposed quarterly
Zoom meetings and sought people with journalism, media or communications
experience. Nominations were due September 18, 2026.
[NJ PBS nomination notice](https://www.njpbs.org/posts/join-the-nj-pbs-ethics-and-journalistic-standards-committee/).

The notice is evidence of a public recruitment process. It does not identify
appointed members or confirm that the committee has met. The deadline is a
historical date, not an invitation to submit a current application.

## Public discussion

The [New Jersey chapter of the Society of Professional Journalists](nj-spj-open-letter.md)
addressed editorial independence, journalism ethics and statewide coverage in
its June 24 open letter about the transition.

In a September 10 *New Jersey Monitor* commentary, Alejandro J. Ramos argued that
Montclair's combined responsibilities for NJ PBS and NJ Spotlight made strong
editorial safeguards especially important. He emphasized journalists' ability
to scrutinize the university itself and called for independent ethics oversight
and a clear way to raise complaints about interference. This was a published
opinion about the arrangement, not a finding that interference had occurred.
[Read the commentary](https://newjerseymonitor.com/2026/09/10/montclair-state-university-nj-pbs/).
