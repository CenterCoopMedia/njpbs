# 2026 NJ PBS operator transition

[Montclair State University](montclair-ccm.md) became the operator of
[NJ PBS](nj-pbs.md) on July 1, 2026, succeeding
[WNET's Public Media NJ](wnet-pmnj.md). In its July 1 message to viewers, NJ PBS
said the university would use the next three months to recruit staff and prepare
new programming. It told audiences they could continue watching during this
period. The change of operator therefore began a transition whose later
milestones included a new newsroom and daily broadcast.
[NJ PBS public transition newsletter](https://mailchi.mp/67a85f3eee1d/a-new-chapter-for-nj-pbs)

## Continuity of the public service

The [New Jersey Public Broadcasting Authority](nj-public-broadcasting-authority.md) retained the licenses for WNJT in
Trenton, WNJB in New Brunswick, WNJN in Montclair, and WNJS in Camden. Its
agreement with Montclair took effect on July 1. The authority describes its role
as ensuring that the service meets New Jersey's educational and public-interest
needs, including news, state addresses, and election coverage.
[NJPBA public overview](https://www.nj.gov/treasury/njpba/)

## News organization and new staff

An August 12 NJ PBS newsletter announced that the network was acquiring
NJ Spotlight. It described preserving the publication's reporting and making its
archive available without charge as aims of the acquisition. This was a public
announcement of the planned change; it did not establish that the transaction
or archive transfer had been completed.
[NJ PBS acquisition newsletter](https://mailchi.mp/0c023b7b98eb/nj-spotlights-next-chapter-is-with-nj-pbs)

NJ PBS announced six staff appointments in its September 2 newsletter, covering station
leadership, news, programming, audience development, digital content, and media
operations. Ashlee Smith was named general manager and Omnika Thompson news
director. The newsletter described an October 1 news launch and the assumption
of full operational control, following Montclair's July 1 start as operator.
[NJ PBS staff announcement](https://mailchi.mp/f196e3aae768/nj-spotlights-next-chapter-is-with-nj-pbs-883500)

## The October news launch

A September 21 university report described work underway on the campus newsroom
and identified *NJ PBS News with Rob Nelson* as the new daily program, scheduled
to begin October 1. The report described a studio visible to students and staff
through its windows and a setting that would place journalists and producers
alongside the college's teaching and research. It presented this campus connection
as part of the new team's approach to public media.
[Montclair newsroom report](https://inside.montclair.edu/news/meet-new-faces-nj-pbs-newsroom-montclair-state-university)

The newscast [debuted on October 1](../timeline/news-launch-2026.md), with
Rob Nelson interviewing Gov. Mikie Sherrill in the
inaugural program. PBS's dated episode and transcript confirm the completed
broadcast milestone. [Watch the debut](https://www.pbs.org/video/nj-pbs-news-with-rob-nelson-october-1-2026-7tzhbq/).

On the same day, [NJ Spotlight](nj-spotlight.md) published a transition
statement describing the continuation of its digital reporting, brand and
mission, along with public access to the archive. NJ PBS's
[public overview](https://njpbs.org/about/) still describes Montclair's
acquisition as forthcoming. These public statements are not treated here as
confirmation of a closed transaction or completed archive migration.
[NJ Spotlight's October 1 statement](https://www.njspotlightnews.org/2026/10/nj-spotlight-joins-nj-pbs-at-montclair-state-university/).

## Reporting team and public accountability

The September 28 reporter announcement named Sean Sullivan,
Scott Minshall and Asha McKenzie
to cover North Jersey, South Jersey, and the Statehouse and Central Jersey,
respectively. [Station announcement](https://www.njpbs.org/posts/nj-pbs-names-reporter-team/).

The transition also prompted [public discussion of editorial independence](editorial-independence-public-record.md).
NJ PBS invited nominations for an ethics committee with a September 18 deadline.
That notice documents recruitment, not completed appointments.
[NJ PBS committee notice](https://www.njpbs.org/posts/join-the-nj-pbs-ethics-and-journalistic-standards-committee/).
