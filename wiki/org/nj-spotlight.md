# NJ Spotlight

NJ Spotlight is a digital news publication focused on New Jersey government,
public policy and statewide issues. In August 2026, [Montclair State University](montclair-ccm.md)
announced plans to acquire its assets from WNET and continue its journalism
through [NJ PBS](nj-pbs.md). NJ PBS's public description of the acquisition
remains future-tense. [NJ PBS overview](https://njpbs.org/about/).

## Founding and early coverage

NJ Spotlight launched in May 2010. Its founders included Lee Keough, Tom Johnson
and John Mooney. Keough served as editor-in-chief, leading the publication's
daily coverage and reporting team. The newsroom's remembrance of Keough traces
its development as a source of reporting and analysis about New Jersey's public
affairs.
[NJ Spotlight's June 21, 2024, remembrance](https://www.njspotlightnews.org/2024/06/lee-keough-nj-spotlight-news-co-founder-has-died/).

## Partnership with public television

[WNET](wnet-pmnj.md) acquired NJ Spotlight in March 2019. The contemporary
announcement described closer collaboration with NJTV across digital and
broadcast reporting while retaining NJ Spotlight's brand, employees and focus
on policy and politics.
[March 2019 acquisition report](https://www.njspotlightnews.org/2019/03/19-03-04-wnet-acquires-nj-spotlight-fosters-closer-partnership-with-njtv/).

On October 5, 2020, the combined news operation adopted the *NJ Spotlight News*
name. The change encompassed the television newscast and a shared website,
bringing the digital and broadcast teams under the same news identity.
[New Jersey Stage's October 5, 2020, report](https://www.newjerseystage.com/articles/2020/10/05/njtv-news-and-nj-spotlight-combine-news-teams-to-create-nj-spotlight-news/).

## The 2026 transition announcements

On August 7, Montclair announced that it would acquire NJ Spotlight's assets
from The WNET Group and integrate them with NJ PBS. The announced assets included
the name, website and an archive of more than 26,000 articles dating to 2010.
The university said founding editor John Mooney had been retained to guide the
transition. It committed to free public archive access and to preserving
existing article links where technically feasible.
[Montclair's August 7 announcement](https://inside.montclair.edu/news/montclair-state-university-acquire-nj-spotlight-commits-continuing-its-watchdog-journalism-through).

An October 1 statement from NJ Spotlight described the continuation of its
brand, mission, digital coverage and public access to the reporting archive.
It is a later public account of the transition. The acquisition remains
described here as announced: this article does not establish a legal closing,
a completed archive migration or preservation of every historical link.
[NJ Spotlight's October 1 statement](https://www.njspotlightnews.org/2026/10/nj-spotlight-joins-nj-pbs-at-montclair-state-university/).

The previous *NJ Spotlight News* television program ended with a September 30
retrospective. [NJ PBS News with Rob Nelson](../timeline/news-launch-2026.md)
debuted the following day. Those broadcast milestones should not be read as the
closure of NJ Spotlight's digital newsroom.
[PBS final NJ Spotlight News broadcast](https://www.pbs.org/video/nj-spotlight-news-september-30-2026-1790786237/).
