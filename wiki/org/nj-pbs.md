# NJ PBS

NJ PBS is New Jersey's statewide public television service. Its four main
stations are WNJT in Trenton, WNJB in New Brunswick, WNJN in Montclair, and WNJS
in Camden. The [New Jersey Public Broadcasting Authority](nj-public-broadcasting-authority.md) holds the licenses;
[Montclair State University](montclair-ccm.md) became the operator on July 1,
2026. [NJPBA overview](https://www.nj.gov/treasury/njpba/).

## Origins and New Jersey Network

The service began with WNJT in 1971. WNJS followed in 1972, and WNJN and WNJB
in 1973. The stations first used the name New Jersey Public Television and
later became New Jersey Network, known as NJN. Its programs documented the
state's politics, communities, arts, and environment.
[American Archive of Public Broadcasting collection history](https://americanarchive.org/special_collections/njn-specialcollection).

That history survives in an archive of news broadcasts and local programs.
The collection includes *New Jersey Nightly News*, the Latino public affairs
series *Images/Imágenes*, *State of the Arts*, and *Due Process*. Together,
these programs record several strands of New Jersey public broadcasting:
daily reporting, cultural coverage, and discussion of legal and civic issues.
[American Archive collection](https://americanarchive.org/special_collections/njn-specialcollection).

## From NJTV to NJ PBS

NJN ended broadcasting on June 30, 2011. Its successor, NJTV, began the next day
under a nonprofit subsidiary of WNET. The change moved operation of the
television service from the state network to [Public Media NJ](wnet-pmnj.md).
[Current's contemporary account](https://current.org/2011/07/farewell-new-jersey-network/).

The network adopted the name NJ PBS on February 24, 2021. Its announcement
connected the new name and visual identity to the station's PBS affiliation,
while describing it as another stage in New Jersey public television's history.
[PBS announcement clip](https://www.pbs.org/video/vo-nj-pbs-1614197340/).

## Programs and public affairs

The station's online program collection extends beyond the daily news. The
*NJ PBS Specials* catalog includes films about the Pinelands, New Jersey's
history of slavery, and Newark's jazz station WBGO. Other programs examine
maternal health and the racial wealth gap, while concert films feature the
New Jersey Symphony. These archived productions show the range of local
subjects carried by the service, from environmental reporting and public
health to music and historical documentaries.
[NJ PBS Specials catalog](https://watch.njpbs.org/show/njpbs-specials/).

## Public appointments

The [2026 operator change](operator-transition.md) brought a new leadership and
news team. NJ PBS named Ashlee Smith general manager
and Omnika Thompson news director. Appointments
also included Samantha Bennet in audience and
marketing, Walik Cotton in media management,
Deb Finan in programming, and
Brent Sanchez in digital content.
[NJ PBS public staffing newsletter](https://mailchi.mp/f196e3aae768/nj-spotlights-next-chapter-is-with-nj-pbs-883500).

The station also invited public input on its developing news service. A
September newsletter asked readers about their news habits and what they
wanted from a news organization. It said the responses would help shape its journalism.
[NJ PBS public survey invitation](https://mailchi.mp/a8a2855163c1/nj-spotlights-next-chapter-is-with-nj-pbs-883546).

Rob Nelson anchors *NJ PBS News with Rob Nelson*,
which [debuted on October 1, 2026](../timeline/news-launch-2026.md). Its first
broadcast featured an interview with Gov. Mikie Sherrill.
[PBS inaugural episode](https://www.pbs.org/video/nj-pbs-news-with-rob-nelson-october-1-2026-7tzhbq/).

The station announced its regional reporting team on September 28:
Sean Sullivan for North Jersey,
Scott Minshall for South Jersey, and news
contributor Asha McKenzie for the Statehouse and
Central Jersey. [NJ PBS reporter announcement](https://www.njpbs.org/posts/nj-pbs-names-reporter-team/).

[NJ Spotlight](nj-spotlight.md) published an October 1 transition statement
describing the continuation of its digital journalism, brand and public archive.
NJ PBS's [public overview](https://njpbs.org/about/) continues to describe
Montclair's acquisition as forthcoming.
[NJ Spotlight announcement](https://www.njspotlightnews.org/2026/10/nj-spotlight-joins-nj-pbs-at-montclair-state-university/).
