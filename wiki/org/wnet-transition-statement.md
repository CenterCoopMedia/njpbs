# WNET's NJ PBS transition statement

*Serving New Jersey into the future* is WNET's notice about the change of
NJ PBS operator. The THIRTEEN webpage carries a July 1, 2026, update and
answers viewers' questions.
[WNET viewer notice](https://www.thirteen.org/serving-new-jersey-into-the-future/)

## Historical setting

WNET's relationship with New Jersey public television began with the launch of
NJTV in July 2011. In a 2024 *One-on-One* interview, WNET president Neal Shapiro
and host Steve Adubato discussed the need for local reporting alongside PBS's
national and international programs. They identified the nightly NJ Spotlight
newscast and longer interviews as complementary parts of the service. That
local-news role provides background to the 2026 notice's discussion of continued
coverage.
[PBS interview with Neal Shapiro](https://www.pbs.org/video/neal-shapiro-chris-paladino-au7dwg/)

## What WNET told viewers

The notice distinguishes the July 1 operator change from WNET's temporary
programming role through September. It directs viewers to continued New Jersey
reporting on THIRTEEN and NJ Spotlight's digital outlets, and provides contacts
for the university and the state authority.
[WNET viewer notice](https://www.thirteen.org/serving-new-jersey-into-the-future/)

## Relationship to other transition notices

NJ PBS's own July 1 newsletter described a three-month preparation period and
assured viewers that the channel would remain available. Read together, the two
notices distinguish the new operator's start from the later introduction of its
new programming.
[NJ PBS transition newsletter](https://mailchi.mp/67a85f3eee1d/a-new-chapter-for-nj-pbs)

The state authority separately confirms the July 1 effective date of Montclair's
agreement and its own continued role as licensee. That record supplies the
institutional context for WNET's viewer-facing explanation.
[NJPBA public overview](https://www.nj.gov/treasury/njpba/)
