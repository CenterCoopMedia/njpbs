# New Jersey Public Broadcasting Authority

The New Jersey Public Broadcasting Authority, or NJPBA, is the state authority
that holds the television licenses used by [NJ PBS](nj-pbs.md). It is distinct
from the organization that supplies the network's programming. Since July 1,
2026, [Montclair State University](montclair-ccm.md) has served as the programming
and services operator under an agreement with the authority.
[NJPBA overview](https://www.nj.gov/treasury/njpba/).

## Establishment and public role

Gov. Richard J. Hughes signed the New Jersey Public Broadcasting Authority Act
in 1968. The authority describes its creation as a response to inadequate
coverage of New Jersey public affairs by existing commercial and public
broadcasters. It operates in, but not of, the Department of the Treasury under
that act, as amended by the 2010 New Jersey Public Broadcasting System Transfer
Act. [Authority history](https://www.nj.gov/treasury/njpba/) and
[Treasury's June 3, 2026, announcement](https://www.nj.gov/treasury/news/2026/06032026.shtml).

The authority maintains the broadcast infrastructure and participates in the
state's emergency communications plan. Its public-service responsibilities
include ensuring that the operator provides educational and noncommercial
programming, a weekday news and public affairs program, state addresses and
election coverage. These licensee and infrastructure functions continued through
the [2026 change of operator](operator-transition.md).
[NJPBA responsibilities](https://www.nj.gov/treasury/njpba/).

## Stations and public records

The authority lists four principal television stations, with direct access to
their FCC public inspection files:

- [WNJT, Trenton](https://publicfiles.fcc.gov/tv-profile/wnjt)
- [WNJB, New Brunswick](https://publicfiles.fcc.gov/tv-profile/wnjb)
- [WNJN, Montclair](https://publicfiles.fcc.gov/tv-profile/wnjn)
- [WNJS, Camden](https://publicfiles.fcc.gov/tv-profile/wnjs)

Its station list also includes W23EX-D in Sussex, W29EV-D in Hackettstown and
W27EC-D in Belvidere. The authority's own site carries board information and
public meeting notices alongside the station records.
[NJPBA station list and public information](https://www.nj.gov/treasury/njpba/).
