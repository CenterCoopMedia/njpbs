# NJ PBS News launch in October 2026

*NJ PBS News with Rob Nelson* debuted on October 1, 2026, beginning the station's
new daily newscast under [Montclair State University's operation](../org/montclair-ccm.md).
The inaugural episode and transcript are available through PBS, documenting the
completed launch rather than only the advance announcement.
[PBS debut episode](https://www.pbs.org/video/nj-pbs-news-with-rob-nelson-october-1-2026-7tzhbq/).

## Preparing the new newsroom

Montclair became the [NJ PBS operator](../org/operator-transition.md) on July 1.
The station's September 16 announcement naming Rob Nelson
as anchor identified October 1 as the later start of full responsibility for
programming and the daily newscast. It credited the NJ Spotlight News team,
including anchors Joanna Gagis and Briana Vannozzi, with continuing news coverage
during the summer transition.
[NJ PBS anchor announcement](https://www.njpbs.org/posts/nj-pbs-announces-rob-nelson-as-anchor-of-new-daily-newscast/).

A September 21 university report described the newsroom and studio taking shape
inside the College of Communication and Media. Its windows would allow students
and staff to observe a working newsroom, connecting the broadcast operation with
the college's teaching environment.
[Montclair newsroom report](https://inside.montclair.edu/news/meet-new-faces-nj-pbs-newsroom-montclair-state-university).

## The broadcast handover

On September 30, *NJ Spotlight News* aired a retrospective identified in its
opening as the team's final show. It reviewed reporting across the broadcast's
history, including its earlier *NJ Today* and *NJTV News* identities.
The conclusion of that television program was distinct from the continuation of
[NJ Spotlight's digital journalism](../org/nj-spotlight.md).
[PBS September 30 episode and transcript](https://www.pbs.org/video/nj-spotlight-news-september-30-2026-1790786237/).

Nelson's first broadcast the next day featured an interview with Gov. Mikie
Sherrill. Topics included affordability and storm recovery. PBS
lists the October 1 episode at 26 minutes, 40 seconds and supplies a transcript
alongside the video.
[PBS October 1 episode and transcript](https://www.pbs.org/video/nj-pbs-news-with-rob-nelson-october-1-2026-7tzhbq/).
