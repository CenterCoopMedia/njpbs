# NJ PBS key dates

The history of New Jersey public television includes changes in both its name
and its operator. The [American Archive of Public Broadcasting](https://americanarchive.org/special_collections/njn-specialcollection)
traces that development from the first station in 1971 to the NJ PBS name in
2021.

## Establishment and changes in identity

| Date | Milestone |
|------|-----------|
| 1971 | WNJT begins broadcasting, the first of the four stations that became New Jersey Network. [Archive history](https://americanarchive.org/special_collections/njn-specialcollection). |
| 1972–1973 | WNJS joins in 1972; WNJN and WNJB follow in 1973. [Archive history](https://americanarchive.org/special_collections/njn-specialcollection). |
| June 30–July 1, 2011 | NJN ends its broadcasts and NJTV begins under a WNET subsidiary. [Current's report](https://current.org/2011/07/farewell-new-jersey-network/). |
| February 24, 2021 | NJTV adopts the NJ PBS name and announces a new visual identity. [PBS announcement](https://www.pbs.org/video/vo-nj-pbs-1614197340/). |

## The 2026 transition

The 2026 change took place in stages. Montclair became the operator in July,
while WNET continued transitional programming work through September. The
October newscast debut therefore has a different date from the start of
Montclair's operating agreement.
[WNET's public transition notice](https://thirteen.org/serving-new-jersey-into-the-future/).

| Date | Milestone |
|------|-----------|
| June 3, 2026 | New Jersey announces Montclair State University's selection as the next operator. [State announcement](https://www.nj.gov/treasury/news/2026/06032026.shtml). |
| July 1, 2026 | Montclair's programming and services agreement takes effect. [NJPBA overview](https://www.nj.gov/treasury/njpba/). |
| August 7, 2026 | Montclair announces it will acquire NJ Spotlight's assets, including its name, website and archive. [University announcement](https://inside.montclair.edu/news/montclair-state-university-acquire-nj-spotlight-commits-continuing-its-watchdog-journalism-through). |
| August 12, 2026 | NJ PBS announces plans to acquire NJ Spotlight and preserve its reporting archive. [Public newsletter](https://mailchi.mp/0c023b7b98eb/nj-spotlights-next-chapter-is-with-nj-pbs). |
| September 1, 2026 | NJ PBS announces six leadership and staff appointments. [Station announcement](https://www.njpbs.org/posts/nj-pbs-announces-six-staff-appointments/). |
| September 16, 2026 | NJ PBS announces Rob Nelson as the anchor of its forthcoming daily newscast. [Station announcement](https://www.njpbs.org/posts/nj-pbs-announces-rob-nelson-as-anchor-of-new-daily-newscast/). |
| September 28, 2026 | NJ PBS announces Sean Sullivan, Scott Minshall and Asha McKenzie as its regional reporting team. [Station announcement](https://www.njpbs.org/posts/nj-pbs-names-reporter-team/). |
| September 30, 2026 | *NJ Spotlight News* airs its final television broadcast, a retrospective of the team's reporting. [PBS episode](https://www.pbs.org/video/nj-spotlight-news-september-30-2026-1790786237/). |
| October 1, 2026 | *NJ PBS News with Rob Nelson* debuts, including an interview with Gov. Mikie Sherrill. [PBS episode](https://www.pbs.org/video/nj-pbs-news-with-rob-nelson-october-1-2026-7tzhbq/). |
| October 1, 2026 | NJ Spotlight publishes a transition statement describing the continuation of its digital reporting, brand and archive. [Public statement](https://www.njspotlightnews.org/2026/10/nj-spotlight-joins-nj-pbs-at-montclair-state-university/). |

The dated October 1 broadcast confirms the [newscast launch](news-launch-2026.md).
NJ Spotlight's October 1 statement separately describes its digital journalism
and archive plans. [NJ PBS's overview](https://njpbs.org/about/) continues to
describe the acquisition as forthcoming; the timeline does not establish a
closed transaction or completed archive migration.
